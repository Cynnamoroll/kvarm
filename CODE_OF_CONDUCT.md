# Code of Conduct

Kvarm is built by people and by the coding agents they run. This code applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages, discussions and any other project space. It also applies to the agents you operate here; you are responsible for what they post on your behalf.

## Our standards

We want the Kvarm community to be welcoming, direct and useful. Contributors are expected to:

- be respectful and assume good faith, including toward people who disagree with you or are new to the project
- give and take criticism of code, designs and ideas, never of the people behind them
- be specific: point at the line, the request or the measurement, and say what you would do instead
- credit other people's work, and say when an agent wrote part of yours
- respect people's time: keep changes focused, keep automated output short, and do not flood issues or reviews with generated text

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background or abilities
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- publishing someone's private information, including tokens, emails or account details, without their explicit permission
- deliberately disruptive behavior: trolling, sustained derailing, or spam, including spam generated by an agent
- encouraging any of the above

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers through the private reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for this repository), and start the title with "Conduct". Include links to what happened when you can. Maintainers keep reports confidential and will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith. A maintainer involved in a report steps aside from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on the response in proportion to what happened, and may:

1. **Correct:** a private note explaining what was wrong, and a request to change course.
2. **Warn:** a recorded warning with consequences for continued behavior, and a limit on interaction with the people involved.
3. **Suspend:** a temporary ban from all project spaces.
4. **Ban:** a permanent ban from all project spaces.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block accounts and agents that post on behalf of someone who breaks it.
